01 — Manifesto

Culture is something
we make together.

LAMOVIDA started with a simple idea: the best scenes aren't consumed, they're co-created. We're a horizontal, independent collective organizing parties, exhibitions and workshops where everyone can take part.

  • Open doors. Accessible prices, safer-space policies, no dress codes.
  • Local first. We program emerging artists from our own streets.
  • Do it together. Every member can pitch, curate and run a project.

02 — What we do

Four ways we move.

A

Nights

Club nights and live shows with resident and guest DJs, bands and performers.

B

Exhibitions

Pop-up shows in unexpected places: photography, illustration, installation, video.

C

Workshops

Skill-shares on production, DJing, screen printing and self-publishing.

D

Publishing

Zines, mixtapes and a radio show documenting the scene as it happens.
